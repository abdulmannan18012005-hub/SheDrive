Privacy Policy

SheDrive Operations Pvt. Ltd. — Lahore, Pakistan

Last Updated: August 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Introduction

SheDrive Operations Pvt. Ltd. ("SheDrive", "we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of all users — passengers and driver partners — on the SheDrive platform. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, how it is stored, and what rights you have regarding your personal information.

By registering and using the SheDrive platform, you consent to the collection, use, and storage of your personal data as described in this Privacy Policy. This policy complies with the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 (PECA) and applicable data protection standards in Pakistan.

2. Information We Collect

SheDrive collects the following categories of information to maintain a safe, female-only environment:

2.1 Personal Identity Information

  • Full legal name (as registered with NADRA/CNIC).
  • Pakistani 13-digit CNIC number and front/back document images (verified for female gender designation).
  • Pakistani mobile phone number.
  • Email address (for account recovery, OTP authentication, and transactional trip receipts).
  • Date of birth (from CNIC verification).
  • Profile photograph / verification selfie.
  • For Drivers: Driving License number, front and back images, and vehicle registration details.

2.2 Location Information

  • Real-time GPS coordinates during active ride booking and journeys (passengers and drivers).
  • Pickup and dropoff coordinates for every ride request.
  • Route polyline geometry and travel history for completed rides.
  • Approximate location when app is backgrounded (drivers only, while online).

2.3 Device & Technical Information

  • Device type, model, and operating system version (Android / iOS).
  • App version and crash telemetry logs.
  • Network connection type (Wi-Fi / mobile data).
  • Push notification tokens for instant ride alerts and security updates.
  • IP address (logged for fraud prevention and rate-limiting security).

2.4 Transaction & Ride Information

  • Ride history including origin, destination, agreed cash fare, and trip status.
  • Ratings and reviews given and received (1★ to 5★).
  • SOS emergency incident logs and dispatch timestamps.
  • Complaint, support ticket, and dispute records.
  • Driver platform fee settlement statements and bank transfer receipt slips.

3. How We Collect Your Data

We collect information through transparent and lawful channels:

  • Directly from you during registration, profile setup, or ride booking.
  • Automatically via the app (GPS location sensors, background services, network pings).
  • Through your camera when capturing verification selfies or uploading official documents.
  • Via integrated cloud infrastructure (Cloudinary for encrypted media storage, Supabase for PostgreSQL database, Firebase for real-time messaging).

4. Camera & Photo Access

SheDrive requests access to your device camera and photo gallery strictly for necessary verification tasks:

  • To capture your live verification selfie during driver registration (confirms identity match with CNIC).
  • To upload CNIC front and back photos during registration.
  • To upload Driving License front and back photos (drivers only).
  • To upload vehicle photos (drivers only).
  • To attach dispute screenshots when filing support tickets.

Camera access is requested only when you explicitly initiate a photo upload action. SheDrive does not capture images in the background without your knowledge.

5. CNIC & Document Storage Security

Identity documents uploaded through SheDrive are processed with highest security standards:

  • Documents are uploaded to Cloudinary — an ISO 27001-certified cloud media service with AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Document references are stored in SheDrive's Supabase PostgreSQL database behind JWT authorization.
  • Documents are accessible only to SheDrive Admin for mandatory identity verification.
  • Documents are never shared with third parties or other passengers/drivers.
  • Admin document review actions are recorded with immutable audit logs.

6. Location Data Usage

Location data is vital for ride execution, navigation, and passenger safety:

  • To show your position on the map and identify nearby female drivers.
  • To calculate accurate route distances and initial fare estimates via OSRM.
  • To track active rides in real time for safety and turn-by-turn navigation.
  • To share your location with your matched driver or passenger during an active ride.
  • To enable the 1-tap SOS emergency feature, broadcasting your GPS coordinates to our safety team and Police 15.
  • Drivers: background location is accessed only while you are actively online on the platform.

We do not sell, share, or monetize your location data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Location history is retained for 90 days for dispute resolution and safety purposes, then automatically purged.

7. How We Store Your Data

SheDrive uses enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure to store and protect your data:

  • Supabase PostgreSQL: Encrypted relational database hosted on AWS infrastructure (Singapore region).
  • Cloudinary: Encrypted media storage for all document and image uploads.
  • Firebase: Real-time messaging, ride status updates, and push notifications.
  • All API communication between the mobile app, admin portal, and server is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS 1.3.
  • Database backups are conducted automatically and stored for 30 days.

8. Third-Party Services

SheDrive integrates the following third-party service providers to deliver reliable functionality:

  • Supabase: Database hosting (supabase.com/privacy).
  • Cloudinary: Media storage (cloudinary.com/privacy).
  • Google Firebase: Real-time updates & notifications (firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
  • Google Places API: Location autocomplete & geocoding.
  • OpenStreetMap / CartoDB: Map tiles (openstreetmap.org/privacy).
  • OSRM: Open-source road routing engine.

9. Cookies & Web Tracking

The SheDrive mobile application does not use browser cookies. The SheDrive Admin Portal web interface uses session-based JWT tokens stored in browser localStorage to maintain admin login sessions. These tokens do not track browsing activity outside of the SheDrive interface and expire automatically.

10. Security Controls

SheDrive implements comprehensive technical safeguards:

  • Bcrypt password hashing with high work factors — plaintext passwords are never stored.
  • JWT (JSON Web Token) API authentication with real-time token block and suspension verification.
  • Rate limiting on sensitive endpoints (OTP, login, password reset) to prevent brute-force attacks.
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) preventing cross-account data access.
  • Immutable audit logging for all administrative decisions.

11. Your Rights & Data Control

Under applicable Pakistani data protection principles, you possess the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of all personal data held about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and associated records.
  • Right to Portability: Request your ride history data in portable format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke permissions for data processing at any time.

12. Account & Data Deletion Policy

When you request account deletion via Settings → Delete Account:

  • Your account is immediately deactivated and driver status set offline.
  • Cloudinary document scans (CNIC, driving license, vehicle photos) are permanently destroyed via Cloudinary API.
  • Personal identity records (name, phone, email) are purged from active tables.
  • Ride history is anonymized for safety statistics.
  • Financial transaction logs are retained for statutory legal compliance.

13. Children's Privacy

SheDrive is exclusively restricted to adult female users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. Any account registered by an individual under 18 will be permanently terminated.

14. Changes to This Policy

SheDrive may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect regulatory changes. Material changes will be communicated via in-app push notification and SMS. Continued use of SheDrive constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
