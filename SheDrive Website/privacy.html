Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 • SheDrive Pakistan

1. Overview

SheDrive values the privacy and personal data security of every female passenger and driver. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the SheDrive mobile application and web portal.

2. Information We Collect

  • Identity & Contact: Full legal name, verified mobile number, email address, and CNIC number.
  • Location Data: Real-time precise GPS coordinates collected during active ride booking and journey tracking.
  • Driver Documentation: Pakistani driving license, vehicle registration papers, and biometric verification selfie.
  • Emergency Contacts: Trusted phone numbers registered for 1-tap SOS SMS dispatch.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly to operate and secure the platform: matching female passengers with verified female drivers, calculating distances, transmitting live location during trips, verifying CNIC gender rules, and facilitating emergency assistance with Punjab Police 15 when triggered.

4. Data Protection & Non-Disclosure

SheDrive never sells, rents, or monetizes personal data to third parties. All sensitive identification numbers and password hashes are protected using robust cryptographic standards.

5. Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions regarding your data or wish to request account deletion, contact our Data Privacy officer at shedrive.support@gmail.com.
