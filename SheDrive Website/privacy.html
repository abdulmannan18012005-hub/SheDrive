Privacy Policy

SheDrive Operations Pvt. Ltd. — Lahore, Pakistan

Last Updated: August 2026 • Version 1.0
📜

1. Introduction & Regulatory Scope

SheDrive Operations Pvt. Ltd. ("SheDrive", "we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of all users — passengers and driver partners — on the SheDrive platform. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, how it is stored, and what rights you have regarding your personal information.

By registering and using the SheDrive platform, you consent to the collection, use, and storage of your personal data as described in this Privacy Policy. This policy complies with the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 (PECA) and applicable data protection standards in Pakistan.

🗂️

2. Information We Collect

SheDrive collects the following categories of information strictly to maintain a safe, female-only environment:

🪪 Identity Information Full legal name (NADRA/CNIC), 13-digit CNIC number, gender verification images, and live registration selfie.
📍 GPS & Journey Coordinates Live pickup/dropoff coordinates, route polylines, and active trip history (drivers backgrounded while online).
📱 Device & Telemetry Device model, OS version, push notification tokens, IP address, and network diagnostics for fraud protection.
💳 Ride & Payment Records Agreed cash fare history, rating reviews (1★ to 5★), SOS safety logs, and driver platform fee settlements.
🔒

3. Document Storage & ISO-Grade Security

Identity documents uploaded through SheDrive are processed with highest security standards:

🛡️ AES-256 Cloud Encryption Uploaded to ISO 27001-certified Cloudinary storage with AES-256 encryption at rest.
🔑 JWT Authorization Database records reside in Supabase PostgreSQL behind strict JWT role authentication.
🚫 Zero Third-Party Sharing Identity documents are never sold, monetized, or shared with other passengers or drivers.
📜 Immutable Audit Trail All administrator document verification decisions are indelibly logged in `audit_logs`.
🗺️

4. Location Tracking & Emergency Safety

Location data is vital for ride execution, navigation, and passenger safety:

🚗 Real-Time Dispatch Identifies nearby verified female drivers and calculates accurate route polyline geometry via OSRM.
🚨 1-Tap SOS Broadcasting Instant SOS broadcasts live GPS coordinates directly to our Safety Command Center and Police 15.
⏳ 90-Day Auto-Purge Historical trip coordinates are retained for 90 days for dispute resolution, then automatically purged.
🔒 Zero Commercial Tracking We never monetize location telemetry or sell movement patterns to advertising networks.
⚖️

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion Policy

Under Pakistani data protection principles, users have full sovereignty over their data:

🗑️ 1-Click Account Deletion Requesting deletion via Settings immediately destroys document scans via Cloudinary API and purges user records.
✏️ Right to Correction Update inaccurate phone, email, emergency contacts, or vehicle details directly in the mobile app.
