1. Female-Only Eligibility & Verification

The SheDrive platform is exclusively available to female residents of Pakistan who are 18 years of age or older. All users must possess a valid Pakistani National Identity Card (CNIC) with verified female designation under NADRA guidelines. Driver partners must additionally hold a valid Pakistani driving license and registered roadworthy vehicle. By creating an account, you confirm that you meet all eligibility requirements.

SheDrive reserves the right to verify eligibility at any time and to refuse, suspend, or terminate service to any user who does not comply with eligibility criteria.

🔐

2. Account Security & User Conduct

Each user is permitted to maintain only one active account on the SheDrive platform. Account sharing, account selling, or creating duplicate accounts is prohibited.

🔒 Credential Confidentiality You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your credentials and account OTPs.
🚨 Immediate Breach Reporting Notify SheDrive immediately at SheDrive.Support@gmail.com upon suspected unauthorized access.
⚖️ Fair Ride Etiquette Treat every driver and passenger with dignity, courtesy, and professional mutual respect.
⭐ 3.5★ Rating Minimum Drivers maintain 3.5★ minimum; sustained low ratings trigger automatic administrative safety review.
💵

3. Dynamic Bidding & Transparent Platform Fees

Fares on SheDrive are calculated based on transparent distance formulas with dynamic ±5 PKR bidding capability:

🤝 Interactive Bidding Passengers propose bids with ±5 PKR steppers; drivers accept or submit counter-proposals.
💵 Direct Cash on Completion Passenger payments are 100% direct cash on completion with zero in-app credit card deductions.
💼 93% Driver Earnings Drivers retain 93% of fares, paying a transparent 7% monthly platform fee settled via bank/Raast.
🚫 Zero Hidden Surcharges No surge pricing spikes or unauthorized extra charges beyond the confirmed agreed fare.
🛡️

4. Zero Tolerance Policy & Criminal Misconduct

The following actions constitute severe misconduct resulting in immediate permanent account termination and referral to law enforcement:

🚫 Forged / Fraudulent Documents Submission of altered, stolen, or false CNIC/license documents.
🚫 Harassment or Abuse Any physical, psychological, verbal, or threatening misconduct towards any user.
🚫 Gender Misrepresentation Attempting to register or ride on behalf of male individuals in violation of the female charter.
🚫 GPS / Telemetry Spoofing Tampering with coordinates or fabricating synthetic trip completions.
