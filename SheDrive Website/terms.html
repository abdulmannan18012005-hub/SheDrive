1. Eligibility

The SheDrive platform is exclusively available to female residents of Pakistan who are 18 years of age or older. All users must possess a valid Pakistani National Identity Card (CNIC) with verified female designation under NADRA guidelines. Driver partners must additionally hold a valid Pakistani driving license and registered roadworthy vehicle. By creating an account, you confirm that you meet all eligibility requirements.

SheDrive reserves the right to verify eligibility at any time and to refuse, suspend, or terminate service to any user who does not comply with eligibility criteria.

2. Account Registration & Usage

Each user is permitted to maintain only one active account on the SheDrive platform. Account sharing, account selling, or creating multiple accounts is prohibited and will result in permanent suspension.

  • You are responsible for all activity conducted through your account.
  • You must keep your login credentials secure and confidential.
  • Notify SheDrive immediately at SheDrive.Support@gmail.com if you suspect unauthorized access.
  • SheDrive reserves the right to close or restrict accounts that violate these Terms.
  • Accounts inactive for more than 12 months may be deactivated after prior notification.

3. Driver Partner Obligations

Driver partners agree to uphold the following obligations while registered with SheDrive:

  • Maintain a valid driving license, vehicle fitness certificate, and registered vehicle at all times.
  • Keep the vehicle in safe, clean, and roadworthy condition for every ride.
  • Only go online when physically fit and capable of safely operating a vehicle.
  • Follow all traffic laws, highway codes, and road safety rules in Pakistan.
  • Accept rides based on vehicle category matching and geographical proximity.
  • Treat all passengers with professionalism, respect, and courtesy.
  • Never charge a fare higher than the agreed and confirmed amount.
  • Complete mandatory document re-verification when requested by SheDrive Admin.
  • Maintain a minimum driver rating of 3.5 stars — accounts falling below this threshold may be suspended for administrative review.

4. Passenger Obligations

Passengers using SheDrive agree to the following conduct obligations:

  • Be at the designated pickup location at the time of booking confirmation.
  • Communicate any pickup or route changes only through the in-app messaging system.
  • Not request the driver to violate traffic laws, speed limits, or take unapproved risky alternative routes.
  • Pay the full confirmed fare in cash upon arrival at destination.
  • Leave the vehicle in the same clean condition in which it was entered.
  • Not make personal contact with drivers outside of the SheDrive platform.
  • Provide honest, fair, and non-retaliatory ratings after rides.
  • Maintain a minimum passenger rating of 3.0 stars.

5. Fare, Bidding & Payment Terms

Fares on SheDrive are dynamically estimated based on distance (km), vehicle category base fare, per-kilometer rate, per-minute rate, and applicable minimum fare floor. Passengers may negotiate the final fare within the platform's bidding system (using ±5 PKR steppers) before confirming a ride.

SheDrive applies a 7% platform commission on completed rides. Drivers retain 93% of the direct cash earnings received from passengers. Drivers are required to submit their monthly platform fee settlement payments through the SheDrive app by the specified due date each month. Payment instructions and transaction tracking are available in the Driver Payments section.

  • All fares shown and transacted are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR).
  • Passenger payments are direct Cash on Completion.
  • No additional charges, tips, or unauthorized toll surcharges may be demanded by the driver beyond the agreed fare.
  • Drivers with overdue monthly fee statements will be temporarily suspended from going online until payment receipt verification by SheDrive Admin.

6. Cancellation Policy

Ride cancellations are permitted but must be conducted fairly to respect the time and resources of both passengers and drivers:

  • Passengers may cancel a ride request for free before a driver accepts.
  • Cancellations made after driver acceptance may result in an account warning if repeated frequently without cause.
  • Drivers who cancel accepted trips frequently without valid emergencies will be deprioritized in ride matching.
  • In case of emergency cancellations, users should contact SheDrive Support immediately.

7. Ratings & Reviews Policy

SheDrive uses a mutual rating system after every completed ride. Both drivers and passengers rate each other on a scale of 1 to 5 stars. Ratings are anonymous and displayed as running averages.

  • You must provide honest and factually accurate ratings.
  • Retaliatory, false, or manipulated ratings violate these Terms and may result in immediate suspension.
  • You may report a rating you believe is fraudulent to SheDrive.Support@gmail.com.
  • SheDrive Admin reserves the right to review and remove ratings found to be retaliatory.
  • Sustained low ratings may trigger an automatic safety review of the account.

8. Misconduct & Zero Tolerance Policy

The following actions constitute severe misconduct and will result in immediate permanent account termination and referral to Pakistani law enforcement:

  • Submission of forged, altered, or stolen CNIC or driving license documents.
  • Verbal, psychological, or physical harassment or assault of any user.
  • Fraudulent manipulation of GPS location or route data.
  • Creating fake accounts to manipulate ratings or gain unfair advantage.
  • Requesting or facilitating rides for commercial, illegal, or prohibited purposes.
  • Threatening, blackmailing, or coercing other users.
  • Any form of gender misrepresentation or violation of the female-only platform rule.

SheDrive cooperates fully with Punjab Police and Pakistani cybercrime authorities in investigation of criminal complaints.
