Terms and Conditions

Last Updated: August 2026 • SheDrive Pakistan

1. Acceptance of Terms

By downloading, accessing, or using the SheDrive mobile application or web portal, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. SheDrive is an exclusive ride-hailing and economic empowerment platform dedicated strictly to female passengers and female drivers in Pakistan.

2. Gender Eligibility & Verification

SheDrive strictly enforces a female-only policy. All passengers and drivers must verify their Pakistani National Identity Card (CNIC) with female designation. Any registration with male credentials or falsified documents constitutes a direct violation of our safety policies and will result in immediate permanent account termination and potential legal reporting.

3. Direct Fare Negotiation (Bidding Model)

SheDrive provides a direct peer-to-peer fare negotiation mechanism. The platform does not impose surge pricing algorithms. The agreed price between passenger and driver represents the final binding fare for the completed trip.

4. Driver Partner Platform Fee

Driver partners agree to a 7% platform fee on completed rides. Drivers retain 93% of the negotiated fare directly from cash payments received from passengers.

5. Code of Conduct & Safety

All members of the SheDrive community agree to treat each other with utmost respect, dignity, and courtesy. Any verbal harassment, discrimination, reckless driving, or safety violations will lead to permanent bans.

6. Contact Information

For legal inquiries, please contact our team at shedrive.support@gmail.com.
