Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how SheDrive works for passengers and driver partners.

💬 How does fare bidding work?

As a passenger, you set your destination and offer the price you are willing to pay. Drivers nearby see your offer and can either accept it or suggest a counter-offer. You choose the driver whose price and rating you like best.

🛡️ Who can use SheDrive?

SheDrive is strictly exclusive to women. Both passengers and drivers must submit their Pakistani CNIC during registration. Male passengers or bookings made on behalf of men are strictly prohibited.

💵 How do payments work?

All rides are paid via direct cash between passenger and driver upon arrival at the destination. No hidden processing fees or card surcharges.

🚗 What is the driver platform fee?

SheDrive charges the lowest commission in Pakistan — only 7% per completed ride. Drivers retain 93% of their full earnings directly in cash, submitted monthly.

🆘 What safety features are available during a ride?

Every trip features real-time GPS tracking, live ride sharing with trusted emergency contacts via WhatsApp/SMS, and a one-touch Emergency SOS button connected directly with 15 Police.

📲 How do I install the app on my Android phone?

Visit our Download page, tap "Download APK", and open the downloaded file. When prompted by Android, enable "Install Unknown Apps" for your browser to complete installation.
