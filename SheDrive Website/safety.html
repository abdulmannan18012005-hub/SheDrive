🛡️ Zero-Tolerance Safety Standards

Your Safety is Our Single Highest Priority

SheDrive was founded specifically to solve harassment and security concerns for female commuters in Pakistan. Every technical decision in our platform puts women's safety first.

🪪

1. Mandatory CNIC Verification

All users must verify their identity using original Pakistani CNIC cards. Our algorithms verify the 13-digit national identity and confirm female gender status (even last digit) before any account can request or accept rides.

🚨

2. 1-Tap Emergency Police 15 SOS

An easily accessible SOS button is present throughout every active trip. Tapping SOS dials the Punjab Police Emergency (15) directly and dispatches SMS alerts with exact live coordinates to 5 registered emergency contacts.

📍

3. Real-Time GPS Tracking

Every kilometer of your route is recorded and viewable on interactive live maps. Passengers can share a web link of their live ride with parents, guardians, or friends with a single tap.

📑

4. Driver Partner Screening

Prospective drivers undergo background reviews, document audits (driving license, vehicle registration, CNIC front/back), live selfie matching, and vehicle roadworthiness checks.

🚫

5. Zero-Tolerance Policy

Any attempt by male users to register, or any reported misconduct, results in immediate and permanent hardware-level account suspension across our platform.

📞

6. Direct Human Support

Our Lahore-based support team is active and reachable via direct phone helpline (+92 42 111 743 374) and email (shedrive.support@gmail.com) for prompt assistance.
